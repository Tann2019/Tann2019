### What I'm building

**[DisDubs](https://disdubs.com/)**: a group dubbing game that runs inside Discord. Pick a scene, hand out the parts, and the whole voice channel performs it together while the script scrolls. Discord Activities can't access a microphone, so a bot in the voice channel records each performer on their own track. Those tracks get placed back onto the original timeline and mixed down to an mp4 with ffmpeg. It's also on Steam as an Electron app.

- **6,131** servers in the first month · **241,502** members reached · **4,322** servers finished a dub · **100+** paying servers
- Stack: TypeScript, Node, Discord Embedded App SDK, discord.js voice, ffmpeg, Electron
- [Read the case study →](https://tannersteorts.com/work/disdubs/)

**[Gridiron Duels](https://gridiron-duels.com/)**: a real-time fantasy football draft game. Players draft NFL rosters head-to-head or in leagues of up to 16 under timed pressure. Game modes include Classic 1v1, League, VS CPU, Crazy Mode, Time Machine, and Weekly Challenges. It has a battle pass, a cosmetics system, a Stripe coin shop, and a companion mobile app built with Expo.

- Stack: Laravel 12, React 19, Inertia.js, Laravel Reverb, PostgreSQL, Stripe, Expo
- [Read the case study →](https://tannersteorts.com/work/gridiron-duels/)
